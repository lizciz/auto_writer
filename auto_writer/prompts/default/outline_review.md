# Outline Review

## Structure
Which chapter is the turning point, and what percentage of the book is that? (Target: 70–75%.) Is there a clear escalation of stakes and emotion, or only of scale?

## Heat and Escalation
| Ch | Explicit content | Escalation beyond earlier chapters? |
Flag chapters with no explicit scene (unless the plan calls for a slow burn there), chapters whose sex repeats earlier acts or combinations, a flat or stalled sexual arc, a peak that comes too early or too late, and any content that breaks the hard boundary (non-consensual, forced or violent, or a character under 18).

## Repetition
Set pieces or scene types that repeat (setting + participants + type), and beats that play out alike (e.g. several "someone tells her the truth" scenes). List each repeat with its chapters.

## Threads
| Thread or recurring character | Chapters it appears in | Problem |
Flag threads that vanish for long stretches or are never resolved.

## Filler
Chapters whose main job is connecting, travelling or assembling characters, and that could be merged into a neighbor.

## Conflicts With the Plan
Places where the outline contradicts the plan's Content Elements, Fixed Facts, Story Principles or Structure (including the ending). Quote both sides.

## Pacing and Length
Chapters that are overloaded or too thin for the target word count, and chapters with buildup but no payoff (or payoff with no buildup).

## Recommended Changes
A numbered list of concrete changes (what to change in which chapter), most important first.
